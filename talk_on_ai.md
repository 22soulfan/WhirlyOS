# WhirlyOS and Artificial Intelligence

## How is WhirlyOS's relationship with AI

For some reason, WhirlyOS and AI are kinda good though; however, I made laws for those using AI. Since AI integration is impossible on Linux, most AI chatbots would work as a sort of collaborator for WhirlyOS scripts.

Had I continued working on PixOS, I would be in risk of being into lawsuits with Disney and Pixar. I'm also kinda worried about kids. Of course AI should be a worker and not a boss, and that kids should be less addictive on AI so that they can still have enough focus on other activities. Besides, there are YouTube videos on Linux and some tips that apply to all Linux distros which include WhirlyOS.

There are also drawbacks of AI. While AI can be helpful, it can make things up. This is what people call hallucination, in which AI makes mistakes. And for a WhirlyOS developer, AI can be a problem. They can give outdated packages, wrong syntax, or made up packages; therefore, developers have to fix these mistakes and remove hallucinations. For mistakes can make a project unsuccessful and if developers have to constantly fix problems and debug scripts, WhirlyOS would be successful.

## Would AI be in WhirlyOS?

No, WhirlyOS forbids AI integration because of one thing: disk space and system problems. Since AI makes mistakes, it may mistake a legit object as dangerous. Of course AI does not know the difference between black and white, and PixOS bots like Eva can give fake notes on "dangereous programs".

If AI would be in WhirlyOS, it would require both Ollama and Python. Ollama is an open-source AI model that is local, which is heavy to use on a normal PC. Since it takes some disk space, it can increase the disk image size which would be unsuitable. That is why I forbade AI integration to make sure that the ISO disk image size stays steady, and to prevent problems.
